# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct about technical problems and generous with the person.
- Criticize evidence, assumptions, and design decisions, not people.
- Ask clarifying questions before assigning blame.
- Respect safety boundaries and different levels of experience.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately submitting unsafe, misleading, or malicious technical content.
- Repeatedly ignoring maintainer decisions or review scope.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block contributors when
behavior harms the project or its community. Report concerns privately through
the repository owner's GitHub contact.

This policy is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.
